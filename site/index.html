Dalel AI

Data infrastructure for superintelligent models.

In the agentic era, the limit is context. Models can think farther than they can find, compare, and hold the right memory. Dalel builds that memory: faster context management, and data systems shaped for the way agents actually work.

  1. 01

    A database of objects

    Vectors live in a collection, with their own metadata. An object is a subset of that collection: a paper, a statute, a person, a stretch of time. There are many object types, and one vector may belong to several of them at once.

  2. 02

    Find a similar thing

    Filtered vector search remains. The query we are adding is similarity between objects. A case, a body of work, or a period can be compared as a whole, under more than one way of measuring distance between sets.

  3. 03

    Public memory, then model state

    After the database, we will publish collections the world already shares: science, law, and news, open under rate limits. Later, stored key-value caches for open models, so context that has been read does not have to be computed again.

Dalel AI is this company. This site says who we are and what we are building. The argument is written out in the master plan.
